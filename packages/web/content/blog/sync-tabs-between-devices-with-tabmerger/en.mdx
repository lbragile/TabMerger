<QuickAnswer>

Sign in to [TabMerger](/features) with a Pro account. The first computer asks you to choose an encryption passphrase; every other computer asks for it once. Your groups, sessions and backups then sync between them, encrypted in your browser before they leave it, so our server stores only data it can’t read. **Settings → Devices** even shows what’s open on your other computers.

</QuickAnswer>

<InstallCta>The same groups on every computer, readable only by you. Free to start, sync with Pro.</InstallCta>

<Section id="problem" title="Two computers, two sets of tabs">

You research a purchase on the home laptop in the evening. Next morning at work, the tabs are on a computer that’s at home. You rebuild them from memory, or email yourself links. Browser sync helps when every computer runs the same browser and account, but your work machine might run Edge and your laptop Chrome, or the work account might be locked down.

TabMerger sync works across Chrome, Edge and Firefox with one TabMerger account, and carries your organised groups, not just whatever happens to be open.

</Section>

<Section id="why" title="Why TabMerger sync works the way it does">

- **Encrypted before it leaves your browser.** Group and session contents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in your browser. The key is random; it’s stored on our server only wrapped (encrypted) with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). The passphrase itself is never sent anywhere.
- **Mandatory, not a setting.** Every Pro account syncs encrypted. There’s no plain-text mode to switch on by accident.
- **One passphrase step per device.** A new device asks for the passphrase once; after that it stays unlocked until you sign out, so sync doesn’t nag.
- **Saved on the device first.** Everything is written locally before it uploads. Offline, nothing stops working; changes go up next time.
- **The latest change wins.** When the same group was changed on two devices, the most recent change is kept.
- **Syncs while TabMerger is open:** when you open it, every 30 seconds while it stays open, and as soon as another device’s change arrives.

</Section>

<Section id="steps" title="Set it up">

1. On the first computer, open TabMerger, then the account menu (top right) → **Sign in**. Use Google, email and password, or a magic link.
2. **Set up encryption** appears. Choose a passphrase (at least 8 characters, no spaces), type it twice and confirm. Your groups start syncing.
3. On the second computer, install TabMerger and sign in with the same account.
4. **Unlock encryption** appears. Enter the passphrase once. Your groups appear a moment later.

<Section id="devices" title="See what’s open on your other devices">

**Settings → Devices** lists each browser on your account that was active in the last 30 days, with the windows and tabs it has open. Expand one to open a single tab, or **Restore** all of them here. Rename this device so the list is easy to read (“Home laptop”), or remove devices you no longer use.

<Section id="benefit" title="What you get">

Walk away from one computer and pick up on another with the same groups, sessions and notes, in whichever browser it runs. And because the contents are encrypted with a key only you hold, a breach of our database would show colours and counts, not your tabs.

</Section>

<Section id="habits" title="Good habits">

- **Store the passphrase in your password manager.** Nobody can reset it for you.
- **Name each device** in Settings → Devices.
- **Open TabMerger once when you sit down** so it pulls the latest changes.
- **Keep the browser-specific bits per device:** URL rules and settings are saved per browser by design. For other ways to carry tabs, see [moving tabs between computers](/blog/move-tabs-between-computers).

</Section>
