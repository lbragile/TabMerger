<QuickAnswer>

To merge **every** window at once, use [TabMerger](/features): click **Add Group**, choose **Merge with current** in its **⋯** menu to save all your open windows into it, then right-click the group and choose **Open all in new window**. Close the old windows and you’re down to one, with every tab saved in case you want them back. To move a few tabs by hand, select them, right-click and choose **Move tabs to another window** (Chrome, Edge).

<Section id="tabmerger" title="How do I merge every window at once?">

Browsers move tabs a window at a time. TabMerger sees all your windows together in **Now Open**, so it can gather them in one go:

1. Click **Add Group** and give it a name, like “All in one”.
2. Open the group’s **⋯** menu and choose **Merge with current**: every open window and its tabs are saved into the group.
3. Right-click the group in the sidebar and choose **Open all in new window**: one new window opens with every tab.
4. Close the old windows. Nothing is lost: the group still holds them all.

<Section id="builtin" title="How do I move tabs between windows by hand?">

Fine for two windows and a handful of tabs:

<BrowserPanels section="builtin">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

1. Select the tabs: <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>-click each one, or click the first and <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>-click the last.
2. Right-click a selected tab and choose **Move tabs to another window**, then pick the window.
3. Or drag a tab into the other window’s tab strip.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

1. Select the tabs with <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>-click or <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>-click.
2. Right-click a selected tab and choose **Move tab to another window** (or **Move tabs…** with several selected), then pick the window.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

Right-click a tab (or several selected tabs) and open **Move Tab**: Firefox 159 offers **Move to Start**, **Move to End** and **Move to New Window**, but no option for another window you already have open. To combine several windows into one in Firefox, use TabMerger’s steps above.

</BrowserPanel>
</BrowserPanels>

<Catch>each move is one window at a time. With five windows, that’s a lot of right-clicking, and closing the emptied windows yourself.</Catch>

<Section id="after" title="How do I keep it to one window?">

- **Save before you close.** Merge with current takes a second and means any window can be closed without a second thought.
- **One group per project.** If the merged window is still too long, split it back into named groups; our [too-many-tabs routine](/blog/manage-too-many-tabs) shows how.
- **Clear the doubles.** Merged windows often hold the same page twice; [closing duplicate tabs](/blog/close-duplicate-tabs) takes one more click in TabMerger.
- **Keep it all.** To pause the whole set and come back later, see [how to save all open tabs](/blog/save-all-open-tabs).
