<QuickAnswer>

Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>K</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>+<Kbd>K</Kbd> on a Mac) in [TabMerger](/features) and type a few letters: it finds the tab whether it’s **open, saved in a group or recently closed**, and filters like `group:` and `closed:` narrow hundreds of tabs to one. Built in, Chrome’s **Tab Search** (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd>) and Firefox’s **list all tabs** (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd>) search what’s open right now.

</QuickAnswer>

<Section id="tabmerger" title="Search everything with Ctrl+K">

A browser’s tab search only knows what’s open in that browser right now. The tab you need is often somewhere else: in a window you saved yesterday, or one you closed an hour ago. TabMerger searches all of it from one box:

1. Open TabMerger and press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>K</Kbd>.
2. Type part of the title or address. Results show open tabs, saved tabs and recently closed tabs together, each tagged with where it lives.
3. Pick one with the arrow keys and <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>.

Every filter, the ranking and the hide-or-dim view are covered in [search all your tabs with TabMerger](/blog/search-all-your-tabs-with-tabmerger).

<Section id="builtin" title="How do I search open tabs without an extension?">

Each browser can search the tabs that are open right now:

<BrowserPanels section="builtin">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

- Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd> on a Mac) to open **Tab Search**, type keywords and pick a tab. Each row also has a close button.
- Or type `@tabs` in the address bar, press <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> or <Kbd>Space</Kbd>, and type.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

Edge 158’s menus include **Search tabs**, but Microsoft’s keyboard shortcut list gives it no key. With many tabs, <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>1</Kbd>…<Kbd>8</Kbd> jump to a tab by position and <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>9</Kbd> to the last one.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

- Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd> on a Mac) to **list all tabs** and search them.
- The address bar also has a **Tabs** search mode, among its search shortcuts.

</BrowserPanel>
</BrowserPanels>

<Catch>these only see the tabs open in that browser, now. A tab you closed or saved elsewhere won’t show up.</Catch>

More keys are in our [tab keyboard shortcuts reference](/blog/tab-keyboard-shortcuts).

</Section>

<Section id="tips" title="How do I make tabs easier to find next time?">

- **Group by task.** Named groups turn “one of 200 tabs” into “the Work group”. Our [too-many-tabs routine](/blog/manage-too-many-tabs) shows how.
- **Close what you’ve finished,** after saving it. Fewer open tabs means shorter result lists.
- **Rename the tabs that matter.** TabMerger lets you give a saved tab a custom title, so it matches the word you’ll search for.
