<QuickAnswer>

**Free** is a full tab manager with no account: 5 groups, 50 tabs, 3 URL rules and 3 sessions, plus search, auto-save (newest 2 kept), 2 days of Recently closed, and import and export. **Pro** (US$3.99 a month or US$42.99 a year) removes every limit and adds end-to-end encrypted sync between devices, automatic encrypted backups, every auto-save kept and 30 days of Recently closed. If Pro ends, nothing is deleted.

</QuickAnswer>

<Section id="why" title="Why the plans are drawn where they are">

The line follows cost. Everything that runs only in your browser costs us nothing to give away, so Free gets the whole tab manager: groups, search, auto-save, Recently closed, URL rules, import and export, with limits sized for one person’s daily work.

Pro pays for what runs on our servers: syncing and storing your encrypted groups, sessions and backups, and the open tabs your devices share in Settings → Devices. It also lifts the local limits, for people whose tabs outgrew 5 groups and 50 tabs.

</Section>

<Section id="enforce" title="How the limits are checked">

- **On your device:** the group, tab, rule and session limits, auto-save rotation and Recently closed retention are applied by the extension. That data never leaves your browser, so there is nothing for a server to check.
- **On our servers:** every feature that stores data with us (sync, sessions and backups in your account, your devices’ open tabs, share links, setting up encryption) is refused by the database for an account without an active Pro plan. A hidden button is never the only lock.

When you approach the Free limits, TabMerger says so with a banner and offers Pro; it never deletes your groups or tabs to make room.

<Section id="cancel" title="If a Pro plan ends">

You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. After that:

- **Nothing is deleted.** Groups and tabs beyond the Free limits stay on your device, shown with a lock, until you upgrade again or remove others to make room.
- **Your URL rules keep working**, even if you have more than 3; only adding new ones past 3 needs Pro.
- **Sync stops for new changes.** What’s already in your account stays there; in the dashboard you can still open or delete saved sessions and download or delete backups.
- **Everything local keeps working:** search, auto-save (new auto-saves rotate, newest 2 kept), Recently closed (back to 2 days), import and export.

</Section>

<Section id="choose" title="Which plan fits you">

- **Stay on Free** if you use one computer and keep a handful of projects: it’s complete, private by default, and needs no account.
- **Choose Pro** if you work on more than one computer, keep more than 5 groups or 50 tabs, or want backups and a month of closed tabs without thinking about it.

Not sure? Start on Free; upgrading later keeps everything you already have.
