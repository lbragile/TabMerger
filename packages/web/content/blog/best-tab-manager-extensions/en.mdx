<QuickAnswer>

If you work across several windows and projects, **TabMerger** is the pick: it lists every open window, saves them into named, colour-coded groups in one step, searches open, saved and recently closed tabs from one box, and syncs end-to-end encrypted on Chrome, Edge and Firefox. The alternatives focus on narrower jobs: **OneTab** turns tabs into a list in one click, **Session Buddy** keeps local crash recovery, **Toby** and **Workona** build visual workspaces, and **Tab Wrangler** closes idle tabs for you.

<Section id="pick" title="Which tab manager is best for you?">

Start from the problem you actually have:

- **“I juggle projects across several windows.”** TabMerger saves each window into a named, colour-coded group and keeps them apart. Toby, Workona and Simple Tab Groups (Firefox) also organise by project.
- **“I lose tabs when the browser crashes or restarts.”** TabMerger’s auto-save keeps a copy of your open tabs on a schedule, and Recently closed keeps what you closed. Session Buddy, Tab Session Manager and Tabs Outliner are built around saved sessions; our [guide to session managers](/blog/browser-session-manager) explains the category.
- **“I use two computers.”** TabMerger Pro syncs groups and sessions end-to-end encrypted. OneTab’s Backup & Sync, Toby, Workona and Tab Session Manager sync too.
- **“My browser is slow and I have 80 tabs.”** Save the windows into a TabMerger group and close them from the group’s menu. OneTab does this in one click into a list, and Tab Wrangler closes idle tabs automatically.

The [too-many-tabs routine](/blog/manage-too-many-tabs) works with any of them. Developers and researchers have their own setups: see [tab management for developers](/blog/tab-management-for-developers) and [organising tabs for research](/blog/organize-tabs-for-research).

<Section id="why" title="Why TabMerger">

[TabMerger](/features) is ours, built for people who keep several windows of work going at once:

- **Every window at a glance.** **Now Open** lists every open window and its tabs; one menu item saves them all into a group.
- **Named, colour-coded groups** that keep windows apart, with drag and drop by mouse or keyboard, notes, reminders and URL rules that file new tabs for you (3 rules free, unlimited on Pro).
- **One search for everything.** <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>K</Kbd> searches open, saved and recently closed tabs, with filters such as `group:`, `window:` and `closed:`.
- **Nothing lost.** Auto-save on a schedule or once you pass a tab count, Recently closed (2 days on Free, 30 on Pro), saved sessions, and automatic backups on Pro.
- **Private sync.** On Pro, groups and sessions sync between devices end-to-end encrypted, always: the server never holds the key.
- **Easy to move in.** Imports OneTab lists, bookmarks HTML and JSON; exports JSON.
- **Everywhere you browse.** Chrome, Edge and Firefox, in 18 languages, with light and dark themes. No account needed to start.

<Section id="tools" title="What the alternatives focus on">

**OneTab.** One click turns every tab in the window into a list, and the list survives restarts and crashes. It runs on Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari, and its optional paid Backup & Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Uninstalling it deletes your list, so export first if you switch; see our [OneTab alternatives guide](/blog/onetab-alternatives).

**Session Buddy.** Crash recovery for Chrome: a timeline of past sessions, import and export in 15+ formats, and no account. Everything stays on your device.

**Toby.** Turns the new tab page into a board of collections, with spaces per project and team plans. The free plan holds up to 60 saved tabs.

**Workona.** Workspaces for people who live in cloud apps: spaces, tab suspension, autosave and integrations such as Google Drive and Slack, built around an account.

**Tab Manager Plus.** Type to filter every tab in every window, highlight duplicates and limit tabs per window. It can save windows too, in that browser.

**Tab Session Manager.** Sessions with names and tags, auto-save on window close and on a timer, tab groups, cloud sync, and import from Session Buddy.

**Tabs Outliner.** Open and saved windows in one tree you can annotate. Free without limits; a one-time Pro adds shortcuts and daily Google Drive backups.

**Simple Tab Groups.** Firefox only. Switch between groups that hide each other’s tabs, with Firefox container support and JSON backups.

**Tab Wrangler.** Closes tabs you haven’t used in a while and keeps them in its Tab Corral to reopen.
