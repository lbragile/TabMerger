<Section id="what-counts" title="What counts as a duplicate?">

TabMerger compares the **full address** in its standard form, ignoring upper and lower case:

<ComparisonTable
  head={['These two tabs', 'Duplicates?']}
  rows={[
    [<code>example.com/page</code>, 'Same page twice: yes, one is closed'],
    [<code>example.com/Page</code>, 'Differs only in case: yes'],
    [<code>example.com/page#faq</code>, 'Different anchor: no, both stay'],
    [<code>example.com/page?ref=mail</code>, 'Different parameters: no, both stay'],
  ]}
/>

That’s deliberate: two tabs on different sections of a long document, or two searches, are different tabs to you, so they stay. Only true repeats go.

<Section id="builtin" title="Can the browser close duplicates itself?">

<BrowserPanels section="builtin">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

Chrome’s help pages describe no duplicate-tab remover. The closest is **Tab Search** (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd>): type a site name, spot the repeats in the list and close them with each row’s close button, one at a time.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

Edge’s tab menu has **Duplicate tab**, which opens another copy, the opposite of what you want. To find repeats by hand, close them one by one as you go along the tab strip.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

Right-click any tab and choose **Close Duplicate Tabs** to close repeats, or **Close Duplicates of This Tab** for just one page.

</BrowserPanel>
</BrowserPanels>

</Section>

<Section id="prevent" title="How do I get fewer duplicates in the first place?">

- **Search before you open.** <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>K</Kbd> in TabMerger shows whether the page is already open or saved. More in [finding a tab among hundreds](/blog/find-a-tab-among-hundreds).
- **Merge windows carefully.** Combining windows is where repeats meet; deduplicate right after [merging windows](/blog/merge-browser-windows).
- **Save, then close.** Fewer open tabs, fewer copies. Our [too-many-tabs routine](/blog/manage-too-many-tabs) makes it a habit.
