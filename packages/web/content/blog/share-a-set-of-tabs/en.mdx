<QuickAnswer>

The one-click way is a [TabMerger](/features) share link: select your groups, press **Share**, and paste the link. Whoever opens it sees every tab on a read-only page and can open one tab, a window or everything, no account needed, and the tabs are **end-to-end encrypted**, so even TabMerger can’t read them. Without an extension, bookmark the tabs and send the exported bookmarks file, or in Firefox copy all the links in a tab group.

</QuickAnswer>

<Section id="manual" title="How do I share tabs without an extension?">

<BrowserPanels section="manual">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

1. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>D</Kbd> to bookmark the window’s tabs into a new folder.
2. Open **⋮ → Bookmarks and lists → Bookmark Manager**, then **⋮ (More) → Export bookmarks**.
3. Send the HTML file. The other person imports it into their browser (or into TabMerger).

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

1. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>D</Kbd> to add the window’s tabs to favorites, in a new folder.
2. Open Favorites (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>O</Kbd>) and choose **Export favorites**.
3. Send the HTML file.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

1. Put the tabs in a tab group (drag one tab onto another).
2. Right-click the group’s label and choose **Copy links in group**.
3. Paste the links into a message or document.

</BrowserPanel>
</BrowserPanels>

<Catch>a bookmarks file exports *all* your bookmarks, and pasted links arrive as a wall of addresses the other person has to open one by one.</Catch>

</Section>

<Section id="tabmerger" title="How do I share tabs with one link?">

1. In TabMerger, save the tabs into a group (**Merge with current** saves every open window).
2. **Ctrl-click** (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>-click on a Mac) each group you want to share in the sidebar.
3. Press **Share** in the selection bar. The link is copied to your clipboard; paste it anywhere.

How it stays private: TabMerger generates a fresh key in your browser, encrypts the groups with it, and uploads only the encrypted copy. The key goes into the link after the `#`, a part of the address browsers never send to a server, so only people with the full link can read the tabs. Creating links is part of [Pro](/pricing).

<Section id="what-they-see" title="What does the other person see?">

A read-only page with each group and window, the number of tabs, and buttons to **Open all tabs** in a window, **Open window** for a whole group, or open single tabs. They don’t need an account, or TabMerger. If they do use TabMerger, they can open the tabs and save them as their own group with **Merge with current**.
