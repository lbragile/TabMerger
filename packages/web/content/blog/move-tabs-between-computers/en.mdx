<Section id="tabmerger" title="How do I move tabs with TabMerger?">

**Once, with a file (Free):**

1. On the old computer, save the open windows you want to move into a group (**Merge with current** in a group’s **⋯** menu). The import adds saved groups; the file’s Now Open is left out, because those were the old browser’s open tabs.
2. Open TabMerger → **Settings → Data** → **Export JSON**.
3. Copy the file to the new computer (a USB stick, email to yourself, a cloud drive).
4. On the new computer, install TabMerger, then **Settings → Data** → **Import file**. Your groups appear next to any you already have.

**Always, with sync ([Pro](/pricing)):** sign in to TabMerger on each computer and your groups and sessions follow you, in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Everything is encrypted on your device before it’s synced, so the server can’t read your tabs. Pro also shows the tabs open on your other devices and restores them here, and keeps automatic backups. How it works, and what our server can and can’t see: [sync tabs between devices](/blog/sync-tabs-between-devices-with-tabmerger).

More on what the file contains in [how to export your open tabs](/blog/export-open-tabs).

<Section id="browser" title="How do I move tabs with the browser’s own sync?">

This works when both computers use the same browser and you sign in to it on both:

<BrowserPanels section="browser">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

1. Sign in to Chrome on both computers.
2. Turn on **History and tabs**: **Settings → You and Google**, select your account name, then turn it on.
3. On the other computer, open History (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd>) and choose **Tabs from other devices**.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

1. Sign in to Edge on both computers and turn on sync.
2. On the other computer, open History (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd>) and choose **Tabs from other devices**.
3. Edge 158 also has **Send tab to your devices** for a single page.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

1. Sign in to Firefox on both computers, with open tabs syncing on.
2. Right-click a tab and choose **Send to Device** to push it to the other computer.
3. Or open **Firefox View** there and look under **Tabs from other devices**.

</BrowserPanel>
</BrowserPanels>

<Catch>browser sync only works inside one browser, only shows what’s open on the other device right now, and doesn’t keep tabs you’ve closed. For your whole set of tabs, across browsers, use TabMerger.</Catch>
