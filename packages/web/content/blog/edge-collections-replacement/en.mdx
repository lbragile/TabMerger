<QuickAnswer>

Yes, it’s gone: Microsoft says **Collections is no longer available starting with Edge 149** (released June 4, 2026). Your pages weren’t deleted: **Move to Favorites** turned each collection into a favorites folder. The best new home is [TabMerger](/features): export your favorites as HTML, import the file, and every folder in Other favorites becomes a named, searchable group, with notes, reminders and encrypted sync, in Edge, Chrome and Firefox.

<Section id="where" title="Where did my collections go?">

During the retirement, Edge showed two options in the Collections pane:

- **Move to Favorites** (recommended by Microsoft): each collection became a folder in your favorites. Only saved web pages moved; images and notes didn’t.
- **Export your data**: for work or school accounts only.

Personal Microsoft accounts can also find previously saved collections at `bing.com/saves`. So check your favorites first (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>O</Kbd>): your collections are probably there as folders.

</Section>

<Section id="move" title="How do I move my collections into TabMerger?">

A few minutes, and every former collection becomes a group:

1. In Edge, open Favorites (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>O</Kbd>). If your former collections are inside **Favorites bar**, move those folders into **Other favorites**. The export writes the whole Favorites bar as one folder, so folders left inside it would arrive merged into one group.
2. Choose **Export favorites** and save the HTML file.
3. Open TabMerger → **Settings → Data** → **Import file**, and choose that file.
4. Confirm. Each folder in Other favorites becomes a group with the folder’s name, and folders inside it are folded into that group. The Favorites bar comes in as one group of its own.
