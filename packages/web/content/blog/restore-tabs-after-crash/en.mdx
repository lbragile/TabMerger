<QuickAnswer>

Reopen the browser and press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> on a Mac), again and again until your windows are back. If that fails, open History (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd>) and reopen from **Recently closed**; in Firefox use **History → Restore Previous Session**. Still missing? Check **tabs from other devices**, then search History for the pages themselves. Next time, let [TabMerger](/features) auto-save your open tabs on a schedule, so a crash never costs you more than a few minutes.

</QuickAnswer>

<Section id="first" title="1. Reopen the browser, then press Ctrl+Shift+T">

Don’t open new tabs first: start from a clean window, so the browser’s “last closed” list is what you get back.

<BrowserPanels section="first">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

1. Start Chrome. If it offers to restore your pages, accept.
2. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> on a Mac). Chrome’s own crash help points here: it reopens the tabs that were open before.
3. Press it again for each window you lost. Chrome reopens tabs and windows in the order they were closed.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

1. Start Edge. If it offers to restore your pages, accept.
2. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> on a Mac) to reopen the last closed tab, and again to keep going back.
3. Missing a whole window? Go straight to step 2, History.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

1. Start Firefox. If it offers to restore your pages, accept.
2. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>T</Kbd> on a Mac). Firefox 159 wires it to a command named “restore last closed tab, window or session”, so after a restart it can bring back the whole previous session.
3. <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>N</Kbd> reopens a closed window; press it once per window.

</BrowserPanel>
</BrowserPanels>

The full keyboard list is in our [tab shortcuts reference](/blog/tab-keyboard-shortcuts).

</Section>

<Section id="recover" title="2. Reopen from History">

If the shortcut gives you nothing, the browser’s History still knows what was open.

<BrowserPanels section="recover">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

1. Click **⋮ → History**. The submenu lists recently closed tabs and windows.
2. Click a window to reopen all of its tabs.
3. Nothing there? Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd> and search for a page you remember.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

1. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd> to open History.
2. Choose **Recently closed** and reopen the window or tabs you lost.

<AnnotatedFigure src="/blog/save-all-open-tabs/edge-history.webp" alt="Edge History page with All, Recently closed and Tabs from other devices in the sidebar" width={1600} height={611} boxes={[{ id: 'recently-closed' }]} source="edge://history · Edge 157.0.4341.60">Edge’s History page, with Recently closed in the sidebar.</AnnotatedFigure>

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

1. Open the **History** menu (press <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> to show the menu bar) or **☰ → History**.
2. Choose **Restore Previous Session** to bring back every window from last time.
3. Or pick from **Recently Closed Windows** and **Recently Closed Tabs**.

If Firefox itself crashed, it may open a **Restore Session** page (“Sorry. We’re having trouble getting your pages back.”). **View Previous Tabs** lets you untick a tab that might be causing the crash before you press **Restore Session**.

<Section id="devices" title="3. Look on your other devices">

If you were signed in and syncing, the lost tabs may still be listed on another device.

<BrowserPanels section="devices">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

Chrome shows other devices’ tabs in History, but only when **History and tabs** sync is on: **Settings → You and Google**, select your account name, then turn on **History and tabs**.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

Open History (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd>) and choose **Tabs from other devices** in the sidebar.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

Open **Firefox View** and look under **Tabs from other devices**. It needs Firefox Sync with open tabs syncing on.

</BrowserPanel>
</BrowserPanels>

</Section>

<Section id="why" title="Why didn’t my tabs restore?">

- **The startup setting opens a new tab page.** Only the “previous session” startup option reopens your tabs at every start; the next section shows where it is in your browser.
- **They were in a private window.** Incognito and InPrivate windows are never restored.
- **You closed windows one at a time.** Startup restore brings back what was open when the browser *quit*. Windows you closed earlier are only in History.
- **You opened a different profile.** Each profile has its own session; check the profile icon.

</Section>

<Section id="prevent" title="Make sure it doesn’t happen next time">

Turn on the startup setting now; it’s the one change that covers updates and restarts.

<BrowserPanels section="prevent">
<BrowserPanel browser="chrome">

**Settings → On startup → Continue where you left off**. Or paste `chrome://settings/onStartup` into the address bar.

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="edge">

**Settings → Start, home, and new tab page → On startup → Open tabs from the previous session**. Or paste `edge://settings/startHomeNTP` into the address bar.

<AnnotatedFigure src="/blog/save-all-open-tabs/edge-startup-setting.webp" alt="Edge Settings, Start, home, and new tab page, with Open tabs from the previous session selected" width={1600} height={636} boxes={[{ id: 'startup-option' }]} source="edge://settings/startHomeNTP · Edge 157.0.4341.60">Edge’s Start, home, and new tab page settings, set to reopen the last session.</AnnotatedFigure>

</BrowserPanel>
<BrowserPanel browser="firefox">

**Settings → Home and startup → Startup → Open previous windows and tabs**. Or paste `about:preferences#home` into the address bar.

</BrowserPanel>
