<QuickAnswer>

Drag one tab onto another, or right-click a tab, to start a group in Firefox. To keep it for later, right-click the group’s label and choose **Save and close group**; reopen it from **Closed Groups** or the **Tab groups** list. Firefox Sync’s settings have no separate entry for tab groups, so to keep the same groups on every device, and searchable alongside everything else, save them in [TabMerger](/features).

</QuickAnswer>

<Section id="basics" title="How do Firefox tab groups work?">

From Firefox 159’s own interface:

1. **Create:** drag one tab onto another, or right-click a tab and add it to a new group. Firefox asks for a **Name** and a colour (blue, cyan, gray, green, orange, pink, purple, red or yellow).
2. **Collapse:** click the group’s label to fold it to one tab’s width.
3. **Manage:** right-click the label for **Save and close group**, **Ungroup tabs**, **Delete group**, **Move group to new window**, **Copy links in group** and **Share group**.

<Section id="save" title="How do I save a group and get it back?">

- **Save:** right-click the group’s label → **Save and close group**. The tabs close; the group is kept.
- **Reopen:** right-click a tab → move to group → **Closed Groups**, or open the **Tab groups** list (Firefox shows **Recent tab groups** there) and choose the group, then **Reopen tab group**.
- **Delete:** **Delete group** removes it for good.

<Catch>a saved group lives in this Firefox profile. It isn’t a backup and, as far as Sync’s settings show, it doesn’t follow you to another device.</Catch>

<Section id="sync" title="Do Firefox tab groups sync?">

Firefox 159’s **Sync** settings list what it syncs: **Bookmarks, History, Open tabs, Passwords, Addresses, Payment methods, Add-ons and Settings**. “Open tabs” is described as “a list of what’s open on all synced devices”. There’s no separate entry for tab groups, so don’t count on a saved group appearing on your other computer.

For the same idea in Chrome, see [Chrome tab groups](/blog/chrome-tab-groups), which do sync through a Google Account.

</Section>
